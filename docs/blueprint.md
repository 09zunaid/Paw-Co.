# **App Name**: Paw & Co.

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Browse and search for pet food, toys, and supplies, filtered by pet type, brand, life stage, diet, and health benefit.
- Auto-Ship Subscription: Schedule automatic deliveries of pet supplies with options to skip, modify, or cancel easily.
- Personalized Pet Profiles: Create and save pet details (name, breed, age, weight, allergies) for personalized product recommendations. Save for later or make repeat orders.
- Shop by 'Paw-fect For': AI tool powered assistance in tagging products using filters to identify products that would be especially helpful to pets, making shopping and recommendations easier.
- Live Chat/Quick Question Form: Provide immediate help with product selection and pet care advice.
- Blog & Resources: Informative articles on pet care, health, nutrition, and training to establish trust and authority.
- E-commerce essentials: Gift card purchase option.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy Green (#556B2F) for a natural and healthy feel.
- Background color: Creamy White (#FDFDFA) for a warm and inviting backdrop.
- Accent color: Sunny Yellow (#F4D06F) to highlight key elements and calls to action.
- Headings: 'Nunito', a friendly, rounded sans-serif font for headings to maintain an approachable and soft feel.
- Body: 'Inter', a very readable sans-serif font for body text, to provide excellent legibility.
- Use authentic, high-quality photos of happy, healthy pets interacting with products or their owners.
- Clean, trustworthy design inspired by Chewy.com with a more local and community-focused feel, curated aesthetic of brands like WildOne.com, and user-friendliness of Petco.com
